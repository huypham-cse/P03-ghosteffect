\subsection{Emulator measurements}
\label{sec:eval-real}
Campaign~2 on the testbed of \S\ref{sec:impl} ran \RealElemN{} Element~1.6.62~\cite{element_android} trials against Synapse~1.164.2~\cite{synapse}
(\RealElemNofaultN{} without fault, \RealElemRL{} with a lost reply, \RealElemAckedN{} acknowledged then killed), plus \RealTimeoutN{} with a withheld reply. It also ran K-9~Mail~24.0~\cite{k9mail}
against GreenMail~2.1.2~\cite{greenmail} without and with an IMAP Sent folder (\RealKnineNosentN{} and \RealKnineSentN{} trials, with acknowledged and offline controls).
CalDAV ran on Etar, DAVx5 and Radicale (\RealCalTrialN{} trials), and a Synapse transaction-id sequence completes the set (Table~\ref{tab:real}).
Campaign~1 (\RealOneN{} trials, no screen check) agrees on which apps retry idempotently (Table~\ref{tab:realcone}, appendix). Eight Element attempts that left no row and four discarded campaign-1 trials are listed in the artifact. Outside Element $n$ is small (from 2 to 4 per K-9 cell), so these are characterizations. Lags are upper bounds:
one read is a uiautomator dump of \RealDumpLo{} to \RealDumpHi\,s. The host's load average was \RealHostLoadLo{} to \RealHostLoadHi{} on \RealHostCores{} cores.

\para{Idempotent retries per app, and measured redo duplicates.} Element resolved every lost reply exactly once (\RealElemRL/\RealElemRL). In \RealElemAckedFirst{} of them its own same-txnId retry, sent 20 to 100\,ms after the reset,
was acknowledged before the fault (``retry, then fault''). In the other \RealElemFaultFirst, all in sticky cells, the fault came first. Element then re-sent the same txnId (\RealElemFaultResend)
or, without re-sending, reconciled its local echo with the event \texttt{/sync} returned (\RealElemFaultSync). A withheld reply also stayed exactly once (\RealTimeoutOnce/\RealTimeoutN; Element showed ``Sent'' via the echo and re-sent after a 70\,s timeout). Synapse deduplicated retries from the same device id, also across a re-login or a restart, but not from another device or after we aged the row past 24\,h, so $H_{\mathrm{dedup}}{=}24$\,h per device. DAVx5 re-sent to the same URL with \texttt{If-None-Match}, Radicale answered 412, and every event existed
once (\RealCalOnce/\RealCalN, \RealCalKill{} after \texttt{kill -9} or force-stop): idempotent by a client-chosen name. K-9 has no idempotent retry by design: once the final ``.'' is written, a failure is recorded as permanent and later send passes skip the
message, which stays in the Outbox. A follow-up audit logged every tap (\RealAuditN{} valid of \RealAuditAttemptsN{} attempts, \RealAuditWindow\,s each, shorter than K-9's 15-min poll). It saw no new DATA after
``Sync account'', ``Send messages'' or no action (\RealAuditSyncData/\RealAuditSyncN, \RealAuditSendmsgsData/\RealAuditSendmsgsN, \RealAuditNoneData/\RealAuditNoneN),
while recomposing sent a second copy with a new Message-ID in \RealAuditRecomposeDup/\RealAuditRecomposeN. In \RealKlongN{} longer runs of \RealKlongObsS\,s, more than two polls, K-9 sent no new DATA and kept the mail failed in its Outbox (\RealKlongError/\RealKlongN). A Sent folder does not help: K-9 appends there only after a successful
send (from \RealKAppendLo{} to \RealKAppendHi\,s after ``Send''), so a committed mail whose reply was lost never appears (\RealKSentWrong/\RealKSentN). A scripted GUI redo duplicated every K-9 send it
was run on (\RealKRecDup/\RealKRecN, distinct Message-IDs, so no receiver dedup applies) and \RealCalRecDup/\RealCalRecN{} CalDAV events: the ghost-effect duplicate of
Figure~\ref{fig:teaser}a, measured with a scripted rather than an LLM agent.

\para{Evidence families and lag.} Element's first read was $\bot$ in \RealFirstBot/\RealElemN{} trials, mostly because a force-stop relaunch lands off the room (\RealFsFirstBot/\RealFsN); first observed reads
were wrong in \RealFirstWrong/\RealFirstObs, all lag-blind. Late reads were never wrong, so the two measured families, the timeline row and the room-list digest, were never wrong together (0 of the \RealCoObs{} trials with both read, CI from 0 to \RealCoHi\%). Campaign~1's five ``co-failures'' rendered
no contradicting state (\RealOneCoContra/\RealOneCoN) and logged no screen identity, so we re-score them as
no-observation. Stale screens do occur. In \RealPilotStaleDigest{} of \RealPilotN{} pilot trials (not among the
\RealElemN), the room-list digest on a verified screen showed an older message while the server held the copy. In the held-out Element run below, the room list was stale in \RealElPlanStale{} of \RealElPlanStaleN{} committed no-fault trials, yet at the certified deadline the timeline row and the room-list line were never wrong together (0/\RealElPlanCoOne{} committed, 0/\RealElPlanCoZero{} absent). Lag depends on the fault class
(Table~\ref{tab:real}b): a network cut adds nothing over the \RealLagNofaultMed\,s reader floor, \texttt{kill -9} gives a median of \RealLagKillMed\,s (at most \RealLagKillMax\,s), and force-stop \RealLagFsMed\,s,
mostly navigation. No recorded trial was censored (\RealCensored/\RealElemN), but follow-up windows differed by trial, so the \RsimTail\% bound from zero of \RealElemN{} is only a reference
for the lag-tail sensitivity rows, not a measured tail.
\texttt{am kill} after HOME was a no-op for Element (\RealAmkillNoopElem/\RealAmkillNoopElem) but killed K-9 (\RealAmkillKnine/\RealAmkillKnine). Memory pressure does reap it: in \RealElmkN{} later sticky trials \texttt{lmkd} killed Element (\texttt{oom\_score\_adj} \RealElmkAdj, after \RealElmkMbLo{} to \RealElmkMbHi\,MB of random tmpfs data). Element re-sent the same txnId after the relaunch and kept one copy (\RealElmkOnce/\RealElmkN), with a longer lag than after \texttt{kill -9} (Table~\ref{tab:real}b). Under a SIGKILL of the
emulator's qemu process (the guest loses its page cache), the WAL ledger lost no acknowledged row with \texttt{synchronous=FULL} (\RealLedgerFullN{} trials). With \texttt{OFF} it lost
from \RealLedgerOffLostMin{} to \RealLedgerOffLostMax{} rows in \RealLedgerOffViol/\RealLedgerOffN{} trials, and \texttt{NORMAL} lost none only because each test transaction ran in its own process.

\para{The planner on real apps.} We also ran the \alg planner itself, in product mode at $\alpha{=}\beta{=}\RealPlanAlphaPct\%$, on held-out effects whose schedule was fixed before each test.
On Element it reads only the timeline and the room list for the nonce (one family, two views, so this certificate needs no A2) after its class's certified wait from the relaunch. Calibration on \RealElPlanCalN{} scripted
fault-injected labels (\RealElPlanCalOneN{} committed, \RealElPlanCalZeroN{} absent, \RealElPlanClassN{} fault classes) certified both sides in every class, with waits of \RealElPlanWaitNone,
\RealElPlanWaitKill{} and \RealElPlanWaitFs\,s. On \RealElPlanTestN{} held-out messages (\RealElPlanInvalidN{} rig failures rerun) it retried
\RealElPlanRetryCommitted{} of \RealElPlanCommittedN{} committed ones (one-sided 95\% CP upper bound \RealElPlanRetryHi\%), declared \RealElPlanDeclared{} done and asked about one whose timeline read was $\bot$,
retried all \RealElPlanRetryAbsent{} absent ones on a certificate, and left \RealElPlanDupN{} duplicates. On Etar, DAVx5 and Radicale it reads only Etar's search and agenda views (hop~1). No screen shows DAVx5's acknowledgement, so \RealPlanCalN{} labels (\RealPlanCalOneN{} committed, \RealPlanCalZeroN{} absent) certified only absence,
and on \RealPlanTestN{} held-out events it retried \RealPlanRetryCommitted{} of \RealPlanCommittedN{} committed ones (\RealPlanRetryHi\%) and all \RealPlanRetryAbsent{} absent ones, asked about the committed ones and left
\RealPlanDupN{} duplicates.
