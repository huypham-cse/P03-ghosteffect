\subsection{Model-based recovery comparison}
\label{sec:setup}
The default model contains eight app profiles, persistent reader errors, correlated VLM errors and a fallible Monitor.
Calibration uses whole effects; each policy sees the same \SimN{} interrupted workflows.
The user can answer incorrectly, so total duplicate/loss rates include user errors as well as planner decisions.
The guaranteed quantity $P_{\mathrm v}$ instead counts committed effects retried by the planner itself.
We use $\delta=\min(\alpha,\beta)/(10K n_{\mathrm{cls}})$, matching the calibration accounting in Section~\ref{sec:theory}.
Here $K$ counts tested bounds per class, and $n_{\mathrm{cls}}$ counts calibrated fault classes (one when pooled).
Appendix~\ref{app:extra} records the complete model, budgets, confidence intervals and paired tests.

\para{Stress and calibration population.}
The product mode exceeds its CP budget in \StressNover{} stress rows; rules exceed their observed retry budget in \StressRulesOver{} rows (Table~\ref{tab:stress}).
An unlogged lag tail gives product $P_{\mathrm v}=\StressLag\%$; logging that tail raises its asking rate to \StressLagCalAsk\%.
Under common-mode stale history that violates A2, product retries \PopCmCProd\% and joint \PopCmCJoint\% of committed effects.
Fault-injected labels and larger label budgets therefore address different risks (Table~\ref{tab:pop}).

\para{Mechanisms.}
Removing the nonce, hop-1 probe or digest raises product's asking rate to \SimNoNonceAsk\%, \SimNoHopAsk\% or \SimNoDigestAsk\%, respectively.
Using nominal independent-read bounds retries \SimNoCalPv\% of committed effects; declaring when uncertified loses \SimNoAskLv\% of absent effects.
