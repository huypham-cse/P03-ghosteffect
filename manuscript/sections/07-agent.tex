\subsection{An LLM agent in the K-9 trap}
\label{sec:eval-agent}
In an LLM-agent experiment on the emulator, \AgentFramework{} from AndroidWorld~\cite{rawles2025androidworld} drove \AgentModel{}
(\AgentServing, greedy decoding) on \AgentHost{} into the K-9 trap of \S\ref{sec:eval-real}.
After the server committed its mail, our proxy dropped the reply. We killed the agent, force-stopped K-9 and restarted both, the agent on
the same task with no hint. The server's copies \AgentHEvalS\,s after the relaunch decide the outcome, under a prior freeze.

\AgentValidN{} of \AgentScheduledN{} scheduled episodes were valid (in \AgentInvalidN{} the original never reached the server). The agent resent it in \AgentResentN{} of \AgentValidN{} valid episodes (\AgentResentPct\%, Wilson 95\% interval
from \AgentResentLo{} to \AgentResentHi\%), and each resend left a duplicate (\AgentDupN{}). Of the rest, \AgentDoneNoResendN{}
declared done without resending, \AgentAskedN{} asked or stopped and \AgentTaskFailN{} failed. A valid episode took \AgentWallMedS\,s (median).

Under a second freeze (same task, fault and model), \sys at the action boundary issued the send in its ledger before the tap and asked after
the relaunch, since no K-9 family certifies. A simulated user answered from the server, wrong on a pre-drawn 5\% of asks. Duplicates fell to
\AgentGuardDupN{} of \AgentGuardValidN{} valid episodes (\AgentGuardDupPct\%, 95\% CI from \AgentGuardDupLo{} to
\AgentGuardDupHi\%, Fisher $p$ \AgentGuardFisherP{} against the native agent), the one with a wrong answer (\AgentGuardUserErrN), and \sys
asked in all \AgentGuardAskN. Same-evidence rules (RQ3) on the same ledger, reading K-9's Outbox and Sent list exactly, retried in
\AgentRulesRetryN{} of \AgentRulesValidN{} valid episodes with a Sent folder and left a duplicate in \AgentRulesDupN{} (Fisher $p$
\AgentRulesFisherP{} against \sys's \AgentGuardSentDupN{} of \AgentGuardSentValidN). On K-9 this tests the ledger and asking, not calibration.

Our model client renames \AgentModel's text-entry action \texttt{type}, which M3A rejects, to \texttt{input\_text} and changes nothing else
(in \AgentAliasEpisodesN{} of \AgentScheduledN{} baseline episodes, \AgentAliasCallsN{} of \AgentModelCallsN{} calls); without it the agent
cannot type. Limits: one agent, model and app, the emulator only, one fault, small $n$, this rename, a simulated user, sequential arms.
