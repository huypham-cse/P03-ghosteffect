\subsection{An LLM agent in the K-9 trap}
\label{sec:eval-agent}
In an LLM-agent experiment on the emulator, \AgentFramework{} from AndroidWorld~\cite{rawles2025androidworld} drove \AgentModel{}
(\AgentServing, greedy decoding), served on \AgentGpuHost, into the K-9 trap of \S\ref{sec:eval-real}, with an \AgentEmuHost.
After the server committed its mail, our proxy dropped the reply. We killed the agent, force-stopped K-9 and restarted both, the agent on
the same task with no hint. The server's copies \AgentHEvalS\,s after the relaunch decide the outcome. Task, fault, model and scoring were frozen before the run.

\AgentValidN{} of \AgentScheduledN{} scheduled episodes were valid (in \AgentInvalidN{} the original never reached the server). The agent resent in \AgentResentN{} of \AgentValidN{} valid episodes (\AgentResentPct\%, Wilson 95\% interval
from \AgentResentLo{} to \AgentResentHi\%), and each resend left a duplicate (\AgentDupN{}). Of the rest, \AgentDoneNoResendN{} declared
done without resending, \AgentAskedN{} asked or stopped and \AgentTaskFailN{} failed; the median valid episode took \AgentWallMedS\,s.
The suite's text-only T3A resent in \AgentTextResentN{} of its \AgentTextValidN{} valid episodes (of \AgentTextScheduledN),
but only \AgentTextDupN{} count as duplicates: it often omitted the tagged subject. Killing only M3A, with K-9 running, left
a duplicate in \AgentKillDupN{} of \AgentKillValidN.

In a second frozen run (same task, fault, model), \sys at the action boundary issued the send in its ledger before the tap and asked
after the relaunch, since no K-9 family certifies. A simulated user answered from the server, wrong on a pre-drawn 5\% of asks. Duplicates
fell to \AgentGuardDupN{} of \AgentGuardValidN{} valid episodes (\AgentGuardDupPct\%, 95\% CI from \AgentGuardDupLo{} to
\AgentGuardDupHi\%, Fisher $p$ \AgentGuardFisherP{} against the native agent). \sys asked in all \AgentGuardAskN{} episodes,
and the remaining duplicate followed a wrong user answer (\AgentGuardUserErrN). Same-evidence rules (Appendix~\ref{app:extra}) on the same ledger, reading K-9's Outbox and Sent list exactly, decided to retry in
\AgentRulesRetryN{} of \AgentRulesValidN{} valid Sent-folder episodes. K-9 sent the mail again in \AgentRulesResentN{} of them (in \AgentRulesTaskFailN{} the agent timed out before resending), leaving \AgentRulesDupN{} duplicates (Fisher $p$
\AgentRulesFisherP{} against \sys's \AgentGuardSentDupN{} duplicates in its \AgentGuardSentValidN{} Sent-folder episodes). On K-9 this tests the ledger and asking, not calibration.
Our client only renames the model's \texttt{type} action, which M3A rejects, to \texttt{input\_text} (in
\AgentAliasEpisodesN{} of \AgentScheduledN{} baseline episodes, \AgentAliasCallsN{} of \AgentModelCallsN{} calls); T3A needs none.
Table~\ref{tab:agent-arms} in the appendix lists all five arms.
Limits: two agents, two fault recipes, one model, one app, emulator only, small $n$, this rename, a simulated user, sequential arms.
