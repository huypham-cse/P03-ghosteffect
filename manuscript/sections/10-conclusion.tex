\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:conclusion}
Ghost effects are real. After a lost reply, K-9~Mail shows accepted mail as unsent and GUI redo can duplicate it. Apps with an idempotent retry can instead resolve the
fault within the server's dedup horizon. Calibration on whole fault-injected effects makes a duplicate budget certifiable when labels match deployment. Joint certification
does not assume independent evidence families. It remains conservative when the GUI cannot reveal the outcome. Passive labels can break the guarantee, so calibration must
track deployment conditions. The agent evidence in this paper is limited to the evaluated emulator setting.
