\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:conclusion}
Ghost effects are real: after a lost reply K-9~Mail shows accepted mail as unsent, and a GUI redo duplicates effects that the server has already accepted. Apps with an idempotent
retry can resolve the fault themselves, within the server's dedup horizon. The emulator agent experiment shows the same recovery failure: M3A duplicated \AgentDupN{} of
\AgentValidN{} valid K-9 episodes, while the guarded agent asked in \AgentGuardAskN{} valid episodes and left \AgentGuardDupN{} duplicate after the simulated user's wrong answer.
A ledger boundary can turn an unsafe redo into a user confirmation. Calibration of structured evidence on whole fault-injected effects, keyed by fault class, makes a duplicate budget certifiable: use product certification when independence has a structural basis, and joint certification otherwise.
