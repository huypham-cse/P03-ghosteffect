\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:conclusion}
Ghost effects are real: after a lost reply K-9~Mail shows accepted mail as unsent, and a GUI redo duplicates it, while apps with an idempotent
retry resolve the fault themselves within the server's dedup horizon. M3A on the emulator duplicated \AgentDupN{} of
\AgentValidN{} valid K-9 episodes, while the guarded agent asked instead and left \AgentGuardDupN{} duplicate in \AgentGuardAskN, after a wrong simulated answer.
Calibrating structured evidence on whole fault-injected effects, keyed by fault class, makes a duplicate budget certifiable. Held out on Element, the planner certified both outcomes without a duplicate. Use product certification when independence has a structural basis, and joint certification otherwise.
