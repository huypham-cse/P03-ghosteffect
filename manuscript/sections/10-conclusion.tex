\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:conclusion}
Ghost effects are real. After a lost reply, K-9~Mail shows accepted mail as unsent, and a GUI redo duplicated every K-9 and CalDAV send we tried.
Apps with an idempotent retry resolved the fault themselves within the server's dedup horizon.
Whole-effect calibration can certify a duplicate budget when labels match deployment and evidence dependence is handled explicitly.
Passive labels break that link. LLM agents, VLMs, and physical phones remain to be measured.
