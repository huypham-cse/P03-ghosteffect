\section{Related Work}
\label{sec:related}

LIMBO~\cite{li2026exactlyonce} shows why verification alone fails after a late commit even when an API gives exact read-back, whereas \sys reads the outcome from GUI evidence. UndoBench~\cite{sah2026undobench}, RetryBench~\cite{liu2026retrybench} and evidence-guided recovery~\cite{zhu2026evidence} study related API evidence.
Atomix~\cite{mohammadi2026atomix} and related systems settle, log or compensate effects. Checkpoint restore can replay irreversible effects~\cite{khan2026resume,li2025agentgit,zheng2026acrfence,wu2026safe}. RFC~1047~\cite{rfc1047} identified SMTP's final-reply window, whose client-side form appears in K-9. Agent libOS~\cite{zhang2026agentlibos} controls dispatch and audit records and durable execution gives exactly-once inside a system~\cite{temporal_docs,langgraph_durable,burckhardt2021durable,zhang2020beldi,jia2021boki}, whereas \sys decides whether an already externalized GUI effect may be retried and formalizes that two-hop boundary in Theorem~\ref{thm:twohop}.

Mobile GUI benchmarks test robustness and recovery~\cite{rawles2025androidworld,chen2025dgara,bu2026recovering,zhang2026gracebench,li2026phoneharness,reintjes2026legacyworld,hu2026cuarecovery,bang2026lostinrecovery}. AndroidReality adds training-free recovery to perturbed AndroidWorld~\cite{liu2026androidreality}. Coati checks outcomes against host evidence~\cite{ying2026coati}, while VeriGUI verifies effects under noisy GUI responses~\cite{zhang2026verigui}. \sys calibrates GUI evidence to a duplicate/loss budget and asks when no action certifies. Noisy-oracle, safety, rebinding and TOCTOU studies motivate this boundary~\cite{zheng2026tsr,yang2026taskvm,wu2025osoracle,sun2026vericua,lee2025verisafe,feng2026cora,qian2026rebinding,lilienthal2025toctou}.

On correlated verifiers, cross-model votes can approve unsafe proposals~\cite{zheng2026vpcontrol}. Identical screens can hide different states~\cite{liu2026guinotstate}, and stale accessibility structure can mislead agents~\cite{zhang2026believe}. These observations motivate joint certification and our foundations in sequential analysis, information theory, conformal calibration and imperfect-evidence limits~\cite{morandi2026sequential,wald1945sprt,cover2006elements,vovk2005algorithmic,akkoyunlu1975constraints}.
