\begin{abstract}
Mobile GUI agents send messages, mail, and calendar entries by tapping through apps. An interruption after the commit tap can leave the outcome hidden. Redoing such a \emph{ghost effect} creates a new request that nothing deduplicates.
On Android emulators, a lost SMTP reply left accepted K-9~Mail in a permanent Outbox error state. Scripted GUI redo duplicated every tested K-9 and CalDAV send. Element and DAVx5 instead resolved the fault exactly once through idempotent retries within the server's dedup horizon.
\sys combines a write-ahead ledger, accessibility-text evidence, calibrated visibility waits, and asking. Whole-effect calibration can certify product or joint decisions against duplicate and loss budgets. A two-hop reduction hands late commits to apps with idempotent retries.
With measured parameters, joint certification retries no committed Element send while asking \RsimFiveJointAsk\% of workflows with \RsimFiveLabelN{} labels per class. It asks on every interrupted K-9 send. Passive labels break every certified mode on Element, and an unlogged lag tail breaks product certification.
In model-based simulation, joint certification retries \SimJointPv\% of committed effects and asks \SimJointAsk\% of workflows. LLM agents and physical phones remain to be measured.
\end{abstract}
