\caption{A ghost effect and how \sys resolves it, grounded in our emulator measurements (\S\ref{sec:eval-real}; steps marked $^{\textsf{m}}$ were observed in every
trial: K-9 \RealKRd/\RealKRd, redo \RealKRecDup/\RealKRecN, Element \RealElemRL/\RealElemRL). (a) The SMTP server accepts the mail but the reply is lost; K-9 records a permanent
failure, never re-sends, and keeps the mail in its Outbox. Redoing the step (a scripted agent, not an LLM) sends a second mail with a new Message-ID, which SMTP accepts.
(b) \sys records the send before the tap and tags it with a nonce. K-9 is not G2 and its entry failed, so hop~1 says nothing about the server, and the Sent folder is written
by the same client; with no family that can certify, \sys asks instead of resending. For a G2 app such as Element the end-to-end row certifies ``committed'' after a short
certified wait. Icons: Twemoji (CC-BY 4.0).}
