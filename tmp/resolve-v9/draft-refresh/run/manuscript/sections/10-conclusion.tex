\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:conclusion}
Ghost effects are real: after a lost reply K-9~Mail shows accepted mail as unsent, and a GUI redo duplicated every K-9 and CalDAV send we tried; apps with an idempotent
retry resolved the fault themselves, within the server's dedup horizon. Calibration of structured evidence on whole fault-injected effects, keyed by
fault class and joint across families, makes a duplicate budget certifiable: with measured parameters joint certification holds the budget on Element at
\RsimFiveJointAsk\% escalations (5{,}000 labels per class) and asks on every interrupted K-9 send, where same-evidence rules duplicate. Passive labels break it; LLM agents, VLMs and phones remain to be measured.
